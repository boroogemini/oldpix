# Third-party notices

## Real-ESRGAN general x4v3 ONNX weights

OLDPix AI downloads the ONNX model at runtime from the Hugging Face repository
[thisismert/real-esrgan-onnx](https://huggingface.co/thisismert/real-esrgan-onnx).
The model is an ONNX export of Real-ESRGAN by Xintao Wang et al.

The upstream model card identifies the model and weights as BSD 3-Clause,
Copyright (c) 2021, Xintao Wang. The ONNX export repository states that its
weights are derived from the official Real-ESRGAN weights.
